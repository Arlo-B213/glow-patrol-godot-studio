## Audio (synthesized in code; no audio files in the project)

Tool: `src/tools/audio_report.gd`. Renders every SFX and music track at the rates the `Sfx` autoload uses.

- 20 SFX and 8 music tracks render. None are silent, clipped, DC-offset, or start/end abruptly. Music loops seamlessly (first and last sample both at zero).
- **Loudness spread (measured, not fixed):** SFX RMS runs from −11.5 dB (`grow`) to −23.4 dB (`eat`). `eat` and `whoosh` sit about 10 dB under the loudest SFX. Music sits at −17 to −20 dB RMS, quieter than most SFX.
- Not verified: how anything sounds. A person needs to listen before the mix is signed off.

## Balance (design/balance.md vs code)

Tool: `src/tools/balance_report.gd`.

- Outfits 870 + weapons 510 = 1380 stars, matching the doc. Outfit costs run 40–260; weapon costs 60–220.
- Battle payout is `clamp(score, 10, 40)`, matching the doc.
- Daily bonus is 10, 12, 14, … and caps at 20, matching the doc's suggestion.
- Pacing claim "first outfit after ~2 runs": at the doc's ~15 stars per average run, the 40-star outfit takes about 3 runs. Worth a playtest before changing anything.
- The bot's scores (91–100) are far above the doc's observed human best of 16, so the bot is not a stand-in for human difficulty.

## Bot playtest (Alley Brawl)

Tool: `src/tools/brawl_bot_playtest.gd` with `brawl_bot_controls.gd`. A simple policy walks toward the nearest foe (or the boss) in x and lane depth, and punches in reach. It never blocks.

- 8 of 8 seeded runs finished the three waves and the boss. Each run took about 2,700–3,300 frames.
- The bot lost no hearts in any run. It never blocks and keeps moving, so enemies may rarely connect. Treat this as a limit of the bot, not a balance result.

## Bugs found and fixed

- **Crash: degenerate polygon in pet crown sparkle** (`src/scripts/pet_art.gd`, `star4`). The sparkle radius goes to zero during its animation, and the triangulator rejected the polygon. `star4` now skips radii under half a pixel. Found by the bot playtest.
- **Bot-found stall fixed in the bot, not the game:** the bot wouldn't advance once a wave was cleared. Nothing in the game needed changing.
