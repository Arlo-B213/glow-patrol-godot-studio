**One-sentence pitch:** A K-pop idol demon-hunting game for kids, where tapping, brawling, rhythm and study mini-games earn Star Points to dress up your hunter and befriend the Lanternboys.

## Core loop
- Play a mini-game, battle, or study quiz → earn Star Points (and XP/level).
- Spend Star Points in the Clubhouse on outfits, weapons, hair and accessories.
- High-five the Lanternboys in the Lounge → affection (+4–8, cap 100) and +2 ⭐.
- Return to HQ, see level/stats, repeat.
