# Story Bible: Glowstick Patrol

> Draft for author approval. Every entry carries a canon level: **Established**, **Provisional**, or **Under Review**. Nothing ships to code until the author approves the act it belongs to.

## Premise (Established)

The city's lanterns are going dark, one street at a time. Gloomlings creep out of the dark where the light went. The player is a new hunter who joins the Glowstick Patrol to bring the light back.

## World rules (Established)

- Light is shared. A lantern gets brighter the more people use it together.
- Gloomlings are born where light has gone unused or unkind. Kindness and dancing make them scatter.
- Nobody can stay in the dark for long without getting cold. This is why the dark needs light.
- Lamplighters are anyone who shares their light. Nobody has to be old or important to be one.

## Factions

- **Glowstick Patrol** (Established): the player's team, led by Hae.
- **Gloomlings** (Established): small shadowy creatures that follow the cold. Each one hums a little tune when it is sad.
- **The Grumble King** (Established, author-approved): the main antagonist for the whole saga. He is cold and lonely, and he took the lanterns to feel warm. He stays the villain; the player learns to be gentle with him, but he is not redeemed.

## The mystery and its true answer (Under Review)

*Why are the lanterns going dark?*

True answer (author to confirm): the Grumble King was once the city's first lamplighter. When the city forgot him, his own light went out. He started collecting lanterns to warm himself, which made the streets darker for everyone. The lanterns were never lost. They were taken by someone who was cold.

Decided by the author: the Grumble King stays the antagonist and is not redeemed. The player learns to be gentle with him.
