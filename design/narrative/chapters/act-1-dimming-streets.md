# Act 1: The Dimming Streets

Chapter lines are the speaker's dialogue. Each line must stay under 120 characters. Quiz grade and rewards are placeholders for balance review.

## Chapter 1: The Missing Lantern
- **District:** Pudding Lane
- **Trigger:** first Lounge visit
- **Quiz grade:** 1
- **Reward:** Lamplighter Robe (outfit)

**Hae:** Look at this, patrol. A lantern-shaped gap on the shelf. Mine used to hang right here.
**Hae:** The Gloomlings didn't break anything. They just borrowed the glow. Very rudely.
**Hae:** I'm not angry. Okay, a little. Mostly I worry the lantern is sad in the dark.
**Hae:** Come with me. We'll follow the little glittery crumbs down Pudding Lane.

## Chapter 2: The Dancing Clue
- **District:** the old bridge
- **Trigger:** win one brawl
- **Quiz grade:** 2
- **Reward:** Star Points

**Bit:** Shh, listen. The Gloomlings hum when they carry a lantern. It goes la-la-LOOO.
**Bit:** I matched the tune with my feet and they followed me straight to the old bridge!
**Bit:** They're not so scary when they dance. Mostly they trip.
**Bit:** Clue found: three little lantern hooks hidden under the bridge. Encore?

## Chapter 3: The Rooftop Promise
- **District:** Pudding Lane rooftops
- **Trigger:** Hae's affection reaches 20
- **Quiz grade:** 3
- **Reward:** Hae's friendship badge

**Hae:** Up here you can see the whole street. Every dark lamp is a promise we haven't kept yet.
**Hae:** When I was small, a lamplighter lit my street. I wanted to be one forever.
**Hae:** Now I think a lamplighter is anyone who shares their light. That's you too.
**Hae:** Tomorrow we find the Grumble King. Tonight, let's watch the stars come out.

## Chapter 4: What the Moths Said
- **District:** Moth Garden gate
- **Trigger:** Nun's affection reaches 20
- **Quiz grade:** 3
- **Reward:** Nun's friendship badge

**Nun:** The moths told me a secret. The lanterns aren't lost. They're in a cold tower.
**Nun:** A gloomy, grumbly tower with one tiny window that faces the moon.
**Nun:** Moths don't lie, but they do get distracted by lamps. I had to ask three times.
**Nun:** Thank you for listening. Not many people wait for the whole story.

## Chapter 5: Stage Light
- **District:** the Shining Ring (preview only)
- **Trigger:** Bit's affection reaches 20
- **Quiz grade:** 4
- **Reward:** Bit's friendship badge

**Bit:** I get nervous before big encores, you know. My knees do a wobble.
**Bit:** Then I remember the lanterns. Every stolen light is waiting for a happy song.
**Bit:** So I'll dance on the rooftop tomorrow. The loudest, happiest step I've got.
**Bit:** You keep the beat, patrol. Together we're a ten out of ten.

## Act 1 end: The Grumble Tower (preview)
- **Trigger:** beat the Grumble King's first encounter
- **Quiz grade:** 5
- **Reward:** unlocks Act 2 and Fellow Hunter A (to be named)

**Grumble King:** Who keeps climbing my stairs? Go home, little light. It is cold up here.
**Hae:** We're not leaving. We brought the whole street's light with us.
