# Gloomlings

## Role and canon
- **Role:** the small shadowy creatures that follow the cold and carry the stolen light. (Established, story bible)
- **Pronouns:** not applicable; they are not given pronouns in the story. (Established)
- **Species:** Gloomlings are a family. Each type has its own look and speed. (Provisional)

## Backstory and motivation
- Born where light has gone unused or unkind. (Established, world rules)
- Carry the glow they took from the lanterns, and hum a little tune when they are sad. (Established, chapter 2)
- They are not evil. They follow the cold because it is the only warm thing they know. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- No spoken lines. Gloomlings hum, and the hum tells you their mood. (Established, chapter 2)
- Scatter when they hear a happy beat. Mostly they trip when they dance. (Established, chapter 2)
- Lines they would never say: none. They do not speak. (Established)

## Visual design
- **Body colours (from the brawl code):**
  - **Gloomling** (basic): teal `#52E8C6`.
  - **Blinker** (quick, fragile): cyan `#6FE7FF`, a flickering shape with a wind-up "!".
  - **Mossback** (slow, tough, health 2): green `#7BC96F`.
  - The Alley Brawl's enemy colour set also uses violet `#8E7CFF`, orange `#FF8B5E` and gold `#FFD36E` for the wider family. (Provisional)
- **Look (Story 031 redraw, `gloom_imp.gd`, `gloom_scout.gd`, `gloom_brute.gd`):** the Gloomling is a small shadow imp kid in a tattered hoodie, with horns peeking out of the hood, pointy ears, a spade tail and big glowing eyes. The Blinker is a lanky hopping scout with spiky hair, one huge glowing monocle eye and a streaming scarf. The Mossback is a hulking brute with a mossy shell cloak, mushrooms on its shoulders and big fists. (Established, code headers)
- **Glow:** a glow inside each one shows which light they carry. (Provisional)
- **Expression:** a wind-up "!" before a strike, and a knock-down when hit. (Established, brawl_enemies.gd and brawl_actors.gd)

## Gameplay role
- The basic enemy of every Alley Brawl wave and the Zap Patrol game. Each type has a different speed and health. (Established, brawl_enemies.gd)
- Gloomlings give the hunter score when they are defeated. (Established)
- Their hum is a story clue: the hum changes when a lantern is near. (Provisional)
