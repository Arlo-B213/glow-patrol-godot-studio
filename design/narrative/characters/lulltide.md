# Lulltide

## Role and canon
- **Role:** the singer of the Gloomling family's nest. A gentle villain whose lullabies make the pets' glow fade, so the pets fall asleep in the dark. Not a main antagonist. Stays a villain. (Provisional, author decision 2026-11-14)
- **Pronouns:** they/them. Not given in the story yet. (Provisional)
- **Relationship to the Gloomlings:** the family's oldest singer. Their hum is the same tune the Gloomlings hum when they are sad, which makes it the source of that tune. (Provisional, ties to gloomlings.md open question 1: a nest and a leader)
- **Relationship to the King:** not his servant. Lulltide sings because they are tired and want everyone to rest, the King's same loneliness in a softer form. (Provisional)
- **Pets in the story:** the pet roster has no story role yet. This sheet is the first to give the pets a narrative place: they are friends of the patrol who can be put to sleep by the dark. (Provisional, needs author approval)

## Backstory and motivation
- Lulltide was the first Gloomling to hum a lantern to sleep, by accident, and found that the quiet calmed them. (Provisional)
- Since then the nest has hummed to keep things quiet, and the pets nearby went dim. (Provisional)
- Motivation: rest. Lulltide thinks everyone is happier when they are quiet and not chasing lights. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Soft, slow, sleepy. Speaks rarely and in gentle, drawn-out lines. (Provisional)
- Hums before every line, so the player hears the mood first. (Provisional, ties to the Gloomling hum rule)
- Sample lines, under 120 characters each:
  - "Shhh... the little glow-moth is tired. Let it rest, dear. The dark is nice and quiet." (Provisional)
  - "I only hum so everyone can sleep. Would you like to sleep too? It is so warm down here." (Provisional)
- Lines they would never say: anything loud, cruel or gleeful. Lulltide never hurts a pet. They only make it sleepy. (Provisional)

## Visual design
- **Build:** a small, round Gloomling elder in a long shawl, with a soft glow inside, drowsy eyes and a little hum-note floating above. (Provisional)
- **Colours:** shawl deep violet `#8E7CFF`, glow teal `#52E8C6`, hum-notes pale lavender `#C9B8FF`. Existing Gloomling colours, so the family reads as one. (Provisional, needs a code check)
- **Look rule:** menacing (author direction 2026-11-14): hooded, cold teal eyes narrowed under the hood, a sinister smile, dark claws on a lantern. Personality stays soft and sleepy. Never gory, never frightening to small children. (Provisional, art bible pillar 4)

## Gameplay role
- Act 2 or act 3 encounter, not a brawl boss. Lulltide hums, and the pets around the player dim. The player must wake them with a rhythm (dance) to brighten their glow again. (Provisional, needs design)
- Lulltide is defeated by waking the pets, not by hitting. They fall asleep smiling. (Provisional)
- Pets never take damage from Lulltide. The effect is only a dim glow that a dance clears. (Provisional)
