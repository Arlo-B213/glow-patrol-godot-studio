# Nun

## Role and canon
- **Role:** Lanternboy and the quiet one. Talks to the moths. (Established, game data)
- **Unlock:** playable after chapter 4 "What the Moths Said" is finished. (Established in code)
- **Pronouns:** he/him. (Provisional, author decision: Lanternboys are boys)

## Backstory and motivation
- Listens to moths, and believes they know where the light went. (Established, chapter 4)
- Counted forty-two lamplighter benches in the Quiet Park. Only one is still lit, and it is cold. (Established, chapter 8)
- Wants to understand the Grumble King, and believes he can be reached with gentleness. (Established: the King stays the antagonist, but the player learns to be gentle with him)

## Personality and speech
- Gentle, slow, thoughtful. Short sentences. (Established, story bible)
- Pauses often. Ellipses are part of his voice: "..." is a complete reply.
- Sample lines (game data): "The moths said a storm is coming. I believe them more than the weather app." and "I counted 212 stars tonight. You were the 213th good thing."
- Lines he would never say: anything loud or quick. He says what he means in as few words as possible.

## Visual design (redesigned 2026-11-14, author request)
- **Build:** slim and still, with careful, unhurried movements. (Provisional)
- **Hair:** short, soft moss-green hair, tousled, with a small plain silver clip. No ponytail. (Provisional, replaces the old silver ponytail)
- **Glasses:** round, thin frames. (Provisional, kept)
- **Outfit:** a cream hooded cardigan over a mint-teal scarf, covered and cosy. (Provisional, replaces the teal robe)
- **Accessory:** a small moth resting on the shoulder, pale gold wings. (Established motif, kept)
- **Glow accent:** lavender `#8E7CFF`, the soft light of the moths.
- **Signature weapon:** a slim rod with a soft lavender glow, used to point more than to fight. (Provisional)

## Gameplay role
- **Brawl move:** Moth Ward, shown on the MOVE button as WARD. Restores one heart up to the cap, and zaps the nearest Gloomling. (Established in code)
- Friendship 20 unlocks chapter 4, and friendship 60 unlocks chapter 8. (Established)
