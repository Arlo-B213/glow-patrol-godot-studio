# You (the player's hunter)

## Role and canon
- **Role:** the main character. A new hunter who joins the Glowstick Patrol to bring the city's lanterns back. (Established)
- **Name:** not chosen yet. The game calls the hunter "hunter" in dialogue. (Under Review)
- **Pronouns:** she/her. (Established, author decision: all hunters are female)

## Backstory and motivation
- New to the city. Arrives just as the streetlamps start going dark on Pudding Lane.
- Wants to help the Lanternboys and find out why the light is going out.
- Grows from a new recruit who is shy about being seen to a hunter who leads with a steady beat. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Curious and brave, a little shy at first. Says "we" more than "I". (Provisional, from the story bible)
- Learns by asking. Her lines are short questions or short promises, not speeches.
- Never mocks the Gloomlings. Her instinct is to light the way, not to fight just for the fight.
- Lines she would never say: anything cruel to the Grumble King. She can be firm with him, not unkind.
- The player writes her lines only through choices, which the game does not offer yet.

## Visual design
- **Build:** about three heads tall, stylised, slightly longer legs. (Established, art bible)
- **Hair:** a long ponytail, in deep purple `#3B2A6B` in the default look. Hair colours and styles can change in the Closet.
- **Default outfit:** the Gloom Breaker, violet `#6C5CE7`, with a track-style cut.
- **Glow accent:** idol pink `#FF5FA2`, the same pink as the UI accent.
- **Signature weapon:** a glowstick. Its colour and reach change with the equipped weapon.
- **Signature look:** a lit glowstick held in both hands. The spotlight behind her is part of the identity.
- The player can dress her in any unlocked outfit (Closet). The default look is the reference for everything else.

## Gameplay role
- Playable from the start. Every brawl, game and quiz uses her look unless a fellow hunter is picked.
- Earns Star Points for the player by winning patrols, games and quizzes.
- Not a Lanternboy, so she has no brawl move. Her strength is the combo chain. (Established)
