# Hae

## Role and canon
- **Role:** Lanternboy and the patrol's leader. Carries the biggest lantern on the street. (Established, game data)
- **Unlock:** playable after chapter 3 "The Rooftop Promise" is finished. (Established in code)
- **Pronouns:** he/him. (Provisional: the Lanternboys are boys, author decision)

## Backstory and motivation
- Grew up on Pudding Lane. A lamplighter lit his street when he was small, and he wanted to be one forever. (Established, chapter 3)
- Believes a lamplighter is anyone who shares their light. He leads the patrol because someone has to go first.
- Wants the lanterns back so the street can be warm again, and worries about the Grumble King's cold. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Warm, proud, a bit dramatic. Loves big speeches. (Established, story bible)
- Polishes his lantern when he is nervous. Says "patrol" as a greeting.
- Makes promises and keeps them, even small ones.
- Sample lines (game data): "I polished my lantern for an hour. An HOUR. It's very shiny now." and "If a Gloomling asks you to dance, say no. They cheat."
- Lines he would never say: anything that gives up on the street. He always has a plan for the next step.

## Visual design
- **Build:** tall and broad-shouldered, the tallest of the three boys, with a confident stance. (Provisional)
- **Hair:** dark indigo `#2A1F55`, in a ponytail. (Provisional, matches the code look)
- **Outfit:** gold `#FFD36E` tailcoat, a long coat with a lantern pin on the lapel. (Provisional)
- **Glow accent:** warm gold `#FFC15E`, the colour of his lantern.
- **Signature weapon:** a scepter with a lantern head. He carries it like a staff. (Provisional)
- **Portrait:** the code-drawn portrait shows a lantern-rim light on his face. Keep that light in every new piece.

## Gameplay role
- **Brawl move:** Lantern Swing, shown on the MOVE button as SWING. A long sweep in front of the hunter on the same lane. (Established in code)
- Has a friendship meter. Reaching friendship 20 unlocks chapter 3. (Established)
- Friendship grows from high fives and Lounge missions. (Established)
