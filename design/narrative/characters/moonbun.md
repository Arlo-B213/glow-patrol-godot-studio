# Moonbun

- **Story role:** none yet. The pets are not in the story bible. (Under Review, see lulltide.md)

## Backstory and motivation
- A rabbit that hops under the moon, with long ears that glow like little lanterns. (Provisional)
- Wants to show the patrol the way home when the streets are dark. (Provisional)

- Gentle and calm. Its ears glow when it is listening. (Provisional, the code blurb gives only the look)
- Sounds: soft hops and humming, no spoken words. (Provisional)
- Lines it would never say: anything loud or cruel. (Provisional)
