# Inkydrake

- **Role:** a playable pet companion. A tiny teal dragon that hatches from an egg. (Established, `game_state.gd` PETS)

## Backstory and motivation
- A dragon that was born from a spilled pot of ink on a lantern. Its freckles are the ink. (Provisional)
- Wants to draw the patrol a map of the streets. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Tiny and brave. Its wing-fins are soft. (Provisional, the code blurb gives only the look)
- Sounds: little puffs of sparkles, no spoken words. (Provisional)
- Lines it would never say: anything cruel. (Provisional)
