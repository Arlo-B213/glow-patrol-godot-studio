# Wickmaw

## Role and canon
- **Role:** the Grumble Tower's stair-warden, the first lieutenant the patrol meets in act 3. He guards the stairs and puts out any light that climbs too high. He stays a villain, the King's loyal warden. (Provisional, author decision 2026-11-14)
- **Pronouns:** he/him. (Provisional)
- **Relationship to the King:** serves him out of loyalty, not fear. He believes the King's cold keeps the city safe from the dark that once hurt the King. (Under Review: ties to the true answer in the story bible)
- **Canon check:** he is a lamplighter who lost his light, which matches the world rule that lamplighters are anyone who shares their light. He is not a Gloomling. (Provisional)

## Backstory and motivation
- Once a street lamplighter who lit the stairs for the people who climbed them. (Provisional)
- When the lights went dark, he kept the last lantern for the King, and snuffed out others so the tower stayed quiet. (Provisional)
- Motivation: keep the stairs quiet so nobody climbs up to be hurt by the dark again. He thinks the cold protects people. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Polite, formal, tired. Speaks in full sentences and never shouts. (Provisional)
- Calls the patrol "guests" with a stiff bow, then insists they leave. (Provisional)
- Sample lines, under 120 characters each:
  - "Good evening, guests. The stairs are closed. Please take your lights and go home." (Provisional)
  - "I put out the lanterns for the same reason you light them. I was trying to keep people safe." (Provisional)
- Lines he would never say: anything cruel or gleeful. He does not enjoy the dark. He keeps it because he thinks it is needed. (Provisional)

## Visual design
- **Build:** tall and thin, in a grey-violet coat with a high collar, an old lamplighter's pole on his back, and a snuffer on his belt. (Provisional)
- **Colours:** coat grey-violet `#5A4E7A`, trim pale lavender `#C9B8FF`, eyes amber `#FFB347` (matching the King's glow, so the two read as related). (Provisional, needs a code check)
- **Look rule:** menacing (author direction 2026-11-14): sharp amber eyes under a heavy brow, a cold smirk, grey mist around him. Never gory, never frightening to small children. (Provisional, art bible pillar 4)

## Gameplay role
- Act 3 mini-boss. He blocks the stairs and uses a snuff move: a dimming wave that the player dodges by standing in a lit tile. (Provisional, needs design)
- Defeating him does not end the fight with the King. He steps aside and gives the player a lantern-key to the next floor. (Provisional)
