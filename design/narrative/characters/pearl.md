# Pearl

## Role and canon
- **Role:** Fellow Hunter B. Joins the patrol at the start of act 3 (moved from after chapter 10 "The Tower Gate", author decision 2026-11-14). Playable in brawls through the hunter picker. (Provisional for narrative; code still unlocks at ch10 until changed)
- **Name:** Pearl is provisional. (Provisional)
- **Pronouns:** she/her. (Established, author decision)

## Backstory and motivation
- The careful planner of the group. Reads the old lamplighter records and knows where each street's lamp was. (Provisional)
- Wants to fix the lamps properly, not just chase Gloomlings. Her plan is to relight every lamp in order.
- Has a personal stake in the Grumble Tower, which she believes has the records of the first lamplighter. (Under Review)

## Personality and speech
- Calm, thoughtful, a careful planner. Short, kind sentences. (Established, story bible)
- Counts things and checks details. Notices what others miss.
- Speaks softly, but is never unsure of a plan she has made.
- Catchphrase (provisional): "Let's do it in order."
- Lines she would never say: anything that pretends the plan is certain. She says what she does not know.

## Visual design
- **Build:** a little taller and straighter than Juno. Stands still before she moves. (Provisional)
- **Hair:** long hair down past the shoulders, deep navy `#101033`, with a star clip. (Provisional, matches the code look)
- **Outfit:** violet `#8E7CFF` armour, a lighter, plated look with clean lines. (Provisional)
- **Glow accent:** teal `#52E8C6`, a cool light that matches her calm nature.
- **Signature weapon:** a plain straight glowstick. It is her only weapon, and she polishes it. (Provisional)
