# Cinderwhisk

## Role and canon
- **Role:** a sly rival who turns up at the Shining Ring (act 4) to steal the spotlight from the patrol's big celebration. Not the saga's main villain. (Provisional)
- **Pronouns:** they/them. Not given in the story yet. Use they/them until the author decides. (Provisional)
- **Relationship to the King:** a rival, not a servant. Cinderwhisk wants the city to look at them, not at the King. (Provisional)

## Backstory and motivation
- A gloom who learned that a bright light gets noticed, and a dim one gets forgotten. (Provisional)
- Stole small lanterns to sell back to the city at a price, and never felt bad about it. (Provisional)
- Motivation: be the brightest thing in the city, even for one night. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Cheeky, dramatic, quick. Loves a showy entrance. (Provisional)
- Sample lines, under 120 characters each:
  - "Ooh, a celebration! Shall I light it for you? Oh, I think I'll light it for ME." (Provisional)
  - "Everyone's staring at your big lantern, patrol. Not at mine. Silly, isn't it?" (Provisional)
- Lines they would never say: anything that admits they are wrong, or that they want to help. They are a rival, not a monster. (Provisional)

## Visual design
- **Build:** a slim gloom in a glittering plum coat with a cape of sparks. A small lantern on a stick, held like a microphone. (Provisional)
- **Colours:** coat plum `#7C4DFF`, sparks gold `#FFD36E`, hair pale pink `#FF9EC8`, eyes gold `#FFD36E`. (Provisional, needs a code check)
- **Look rule:** a showy, bouncy idol-villain. Never gory, never frightening to small children. Dark purple neon backdrop, not on the character. (Provisional, art bible pillar 4)

## Gameplay role
- Act 4 rival. Appears during the Shining Ring celebration and challenges the patrol to a dance-off, which is a Lounge-style rhythm mini-game, not a brawl. (Provisional, needs design)
- Losing the dance-off makes Cinderwhisk leave in a sparkle, not in defeat. (Provisional)
