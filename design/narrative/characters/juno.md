# Juno

## Role and canon
- **Role:** Fellow Hunter A. Joins the patrol after chapter 5 "Stage Light". Playable in brawls through the hunter picker. (Established in code, name provisional)
- **Name:** Juno is a provisional name chosen with the author's permission. (Provisional)
- **Pronouns:** she/her. (Established, author decision)

## Backstory and motivation
- A dancer who learned that rhythm can scare the dark away, and tries to teach everyone the beat. (Provisional)
- Wants to show that a patrol can be fast and fun and still keep people safe.
- Wants to find the lanterns' true origin, and is impatient to get to the Grumble Tower. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Bright, fast, loves rhythm. Says "let's go!" a lot. (Established, story bible)
- Talks in beats: short lines, a call and response when she is excited.
- Warm to everyone, but gets restless when someone is slow. She learns patience from Nun.
- Catchphrases (provisional): "One, two, go!" and "Make the dark dance."
- Lines she would never say: anything that makes light sound like a weapon. She talks about light as a gift.
- Speaks about herself in the first person ("I") freely.

## Visual design
- **Build:** the same hunter proportions as You, but a little lighter in the frame to suggest speed. (Provisional)
- **Hair:** gold `#FFC15E`, in twintails. (Provisional, matches the code look)
- **Outfit:** teal `#52E8C6` sequin, with a sparkle pattern that catches the stage light. (Provisional)
- **Accessories:** a bow in the hair, and blush on the cheeks. (Provisional)
- **Glow accent:** hot pink `#FF5FA2`.
- **Signature weapon:** a comet-style glowstick with a tail of light that trails behind each swing. (Provisional)

## Gameplay role
- Unlocks after chapter 5 is finished (quiz passed). Locked before that.
- Playable hunter with the same combo chain as You. No brawl move, because only the Lanternboys have moves. (Established)
- Her look appears in the Closet picker and in every brawl, game and quiz while she is the active hunter.
