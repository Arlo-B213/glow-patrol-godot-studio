# Bit

## Role and canon
- **Role:** Lanternboy and the patrol's dancer. Hums while he walks. (Established, game data)
- **Unlock:** playable after chapter 2 "The Dancing Clue" is finished. (Established in code)
- **Pronouns:** he/him. (Provisional, author decision: Lanternboys are boys)

## Backstory and motivation
- Found the Gloomlings' hiding place by humming, because they follow a tune. (Established, chapter 2)
- Wants to make the dark dance so it forgets to be scary. He thinks no one should be frightened of the dark forever.
- Hopes to dance on a rooftop stage for the whole street one day. (Established, chapter 5)

## Personality and speech
- Bouncy, nervous, funny. Turns fear into dance. (Established, story bible)
- Hums when he is thinking and when he is worried. He gets nervous before big encores, and his knees wobble.
- Sample lines (game data): "Do you ever hum during battle? I do. It confuses them." and "Your outfit today is a solid nine. I'm saving the ten for stage day."
- Lines he would never say: anything that makes a fight sound fun for its own sake. He dances to make the dark gentler.

## Visual design
- **Build:** medium height, springy. His stance always looks ready to bounce. (Provisional)
- **Hair:** short, spiky brown `#8A4B2A`, swept up at the front. (Provisional, updated to the approved reference)
- **Outfit:** orange `#FF8B5E` sequin jacket that flashes when he turns. (Provisional)
- **Accessory:** headphones, shown in the code-drawn portrait. Keep them in every piece. (Established, lanternboy_art.gd)
- **Glow accent:** teal `#52E8C6`, a bright light that matches his dance energy.
- **Signature weapon:** a rod, held like a conductor's baton. (Provisional)

## Gameplay role
- **Brawl move:** Encore Spin, shown on the MOVE button as SPIN. Hits everything close by, on either side of the hunter. (Established in code)
- Friendship 20 unlocks chapter 5, and friendship 60 unlocks chapter 9. (Established)
