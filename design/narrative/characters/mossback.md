# Mossback

## Role and canon
- **Role:** a slow, tough Gloomling brute. It takes two hits to defeat and shows its tough shell in every wave. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
- **Species:** a type of Gloomling. See `gloomlings.md`. (Established)
- **Pronouns:** not given; no spoken lines. (Established)

## Backstory and motivation
- A Gloomling that grew a mossy shell over years in one dark corner, where no lantern reached. (Provisional)
- Its mushrooms are the only light it has, and it guards them. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- No spoken lines. It looks grumpy and stands its ground. (Provisional)
- Lines it would never say: none. It does not speak. (Established)

## Visual design
- **Build:** hulking, with a mossy shell cloak, mushrooms on its shoulders, big fists and a grumpy face sunk between its shoulders. (Established, `gloom_brute.gd`)
- **Colour:** green `#7BC96F`. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
- **Damage state:** when it has 1 health left, the right-hand mushroom is knocked off. (Established, `gloom_brute.gd`)
- **Look rule:** tough and grumpy, never gory. Not frightening to small children. (Provisional, art bible pillar 4)

## Gameplay role
- Speed 0.6 (slowest enemy), health 2, score 4. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
- Found mostly in later waves. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
