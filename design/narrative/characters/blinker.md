# Blinker

## Role and canon
- **Role:** a quick, fragile Gloomling scout. The fastest enemy in the Alley Brawl and the Zap Patrol game. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
- **Species:** a type of Gloomling. See `gloomlings.md`. (Established)
- **Pronouns:** not given; no spoken lines. (Established)

## Backstory and motivation
- A Gloomling that learned to hop, so it can get away from light faster. (Provisional)
- Its one big glowing eye is the light it carries, which flickers when it is scared. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- No spoken lines. Its mood shows in how it hops and flickers. (Provisional, matches the Gloomling hum rule)
- Hops away when it hears a happy beat, like the other Gloomlings. (Provisional)
- Lines it would never say: none. It does not speak. (Established)

## Visual design
- **Build:** lanky and hopping, with spiky hair, one huge glowing monocle eye, a tiny second eye and a streaming scarf. Springy bent legs. (Established, `gloom_scout.gd`)
- **Colour:** cyan `#6FE7FF`. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
- **Expression:** a wind-up "!" before it strikes. Flickers when hit. (Established, `brawl_ai.gd`)
- **Look rule:** quick and a little menacing, never gory. Not frightening to small children. (Provisional, art bible pillar 4)

## Gameplay role
- Speed 1.27, health 1, score 3. Only travels in the air part of its hop. (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`, `gloom_scout.gd`)
- Found mostly in later waves (weights rise after wave 1). (Established, `brawl_enemies.gd`)
