# The Grumble King

## Role and canon
- **Role:** the main antagonist for the whole saga. He took the lanterns. He stays the villain; the player learns to be gentle with him, but he is not redeemed. (Established, author decision)
- **Pronouns:** he/him. (Provisional)
- **Fight:** the finale against him is offered after chapter 10 (act 2). Act 3 raises the stakes. (Provisional)

## Backstory and motivation
- The city's first lamplighter. When the city forgot him, his own light went out. (Under Review: the true answer is in the story bible)
- Started collecting lanterns to warm himself, which made the streets darker for everyone. (Under Review)
- Lives high in the Grumble Tower, alone with the stolen lanterns. (Provisional)
- Motivation: to feel warm again. He does not want to hurt anyone, and does not know how to ask for help. (Provisional)

## Personality and speech
- Grumbly, tired, secretly kind. Speaks in short, heavy lines. (Established, story bible)
- Says "Go home, little lights." He calls the patrol "little lights" as an insult that softens over time. (Provisional)
- Sample lines (chapter 10): "Who keeps climbing my stairs? Go home, little lights. It is cold up here, and I am tired."
- Lines he would never say: a cheerful line, or any line that admits he is happy with the dark. He can be cruel in a tired way, never in a gleeful way.

## Visual design
- **Build:** a regal giant with a crown, bushy brows and a big beard. A flowing cape and big hands. (Established, gloom_king.gd)
- **One hand** holds a cracked, glowing lantern. (Established, gloom_king.gd)
- **Angry face:** a cool, angry-but-sad expression. (Established, gloom_king.gd)
- **Enraged state (health 34% or less):** red eyes, steam, a cracked crown. (Established, gloom_king.gd)
- **Colours:** a deep cool palette so the lantern is the brightest thing on screen, which matches the art bible's "glow is the hero" rule. (Provisional)

## Gameplay role
- Boss of the Alley Brawl. Enters with an entrance pose. Health is 8 in normal boss fights and 12 for the finale. (Established, alley_brawl_view.gd)
- Only hit by the player's punches and by the Lanternboys' moves, which chip 1 each. (Established, brawl_moves.gd)
- Being gentle with him is a theme of the story, but there is no gentle option in the fight yet. (Under Review)
